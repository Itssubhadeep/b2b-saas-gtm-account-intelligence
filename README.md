# B2B SaaS GTM Strategy & Account Intelligence

## Overview
An AI-assisted independent GTM research case study exploring potential background verification use cases among 20 Indian IT services and SaaS organizations.

The project uses RecriAuth by Recrivio as a case-study reference. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Recrivio.

## Objectives
- Research 20 real IT services and SaaS organizations.
- Document publicly available hiring and workforce signals.
- Explore potential background verification use cases.
- Develop a weighted Excel account prioritization model.
- Identify five accounts for further research.

## Prioritization Model
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Hiring evidence strength | 40% |
| Hiring complexity signals | 25% |
| Potential product relevance | 20% |
| Research accessibility | 15% |

Weighted score = 0.40H + 0.25C + 0.20R + 0.15A

Scores reflect research priorities, not actual buying intent.

## Tools
Microsoft Excel, public company research, AI-assisted research and documentation.

## Project Files
- `data/account_research.xlsx` — 20-account research tracker.
- `data/account_prioritization.xlsx` — weighted scoring model and shortlist.

## Limitations
- No outreach or customer conversations were conducted.
- No qualified leads, revenue, or conversion outcomes are claimed.
- Scoring weights are analyst-defined assumptions.
- Public hiring information may change and requires revalidation.

## AI Usage
AI assisted with initial research, structuring, documentation, and Excel model creation. Findings and assumptions should be independently checked before being treated as verified.

## Status
Research and initial account prioritization completed. Independent source validation and outbound strategy development remain next steps.
